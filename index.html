A working ARGUS prototype that launches a real Meta campaign by December 10

ARGUS will be an agentic advertising specialist for Macmillan imprints. The prototype lets an imprint ad operator type an ISBN and a budget, get a recommended launch plan, sign off, and see it go live on a Meta test ad account, then track it with a title health score.

1

What the prototype has to prove

Many people running ads at Macmillan are generalists who also juggle other trade marketing demands, so campaigns get set up whichever way is quickest. The prototype tests whether ARGUS can give them a correct, best-practice setup with almost no ad expertise required.

  • An ad operator goes from ISBN to a signed-off Meta campaign in under 10 minutes, without help.
  • The campaign ARGUS creates in the test account matches the approved plan exactly.
  • Operators can explain what the health score says about a title, and why, after one look.
  • Operators trust the suggestions enough to approve most of them, and can switch to automatic mode when they want to.
2

Scope

In the prototype
  • Real sign-in with marketer, director, and finance roles; each user sees one imprint
  • Launch center: ISBN, budget, goal, flight dates, platforms, destination, comp titles, tone
  • AI-drafted plan with a recommended platform mix and budget split, editable field by field
  • Uploaded static images, with Reedy keywords and copy (simulated) shown and editable
  • One-person sign-off, then a real launch to a Meta test ad account
  • Live tracking, health score, and suggestions with an approve-or-automatic toggle
  • Imprint portfolio view and an on-demand end-of-campaign PDF
Not yet
  • Google, TikTok, and Amazon launches (planned in the mix, not launched)
  • Live CHAPTR integration; Reedy and Amplify are simulated and labeled "powered by CHAPTR"
  • Generated creative, video, and multi-title carousels
  • Real BookScan or Amazon sales data; sales lines use illustrative numbers
  • Chat assistant, phone layout, and Slack or email approvals
  • Saved imprint budget profiles

The three features that matter most

Launch plan

From ISBN to live ads

ARGUS pulls the title, reads the brief, and recommends which platforms to use and how to split the budget, with a reason next to every choice.

Once the marketer signs off, ARGUS launches the ads itself, building every campaign, ad set, and ad in Meta with no manual setup.

Health score

One number per title, 0 to 100

Results vs. goal40%
Open problems30%
Creative fatigue20%
Spend pacing10%

Weights are a proposed starting point.

Live tracking

Real delivery from the test account

Spend, pacing, and results pulled from Meta, shown next to weekly sales. The main result is the change in weekly sales during the campaign.

  • Daily digest per title, alerts only when action is needed
  • Sales are illustrative until BookScan or Amazon data is connected

Suggestions and autonomy

Each suggestion shows the change in one line, why, the expected impact as a range, and the risk, with undo steps and expiry under details. A toggle switches between "I approve every change" and "ARGUS makes the change," so we can test both. Approvals happen inside ARGUS only.
